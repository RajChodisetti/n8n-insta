# Avatar Rules

Use for avatar, presenter, likeness, synthetic host, and consent-sensitive generation.

## Blocking

- Do not use a real person's likeness, voice, or implied endorsement without explicit consent metadata.
- Do not present a synthetic avatar as if it were an unrecorded real-person message.
- Do not use avatar routes for sensitive topics where a presenter would make the content exploitative, deceptive, or unsafe.

## Must

- Synthetic avatar routes must remain transparent.
- Avatar prompts must avoid real person names, customer likenesses, employee likenesses, or celebrity resemblance unless consent and rights are explicitly supplied.
- If consent metadata is missing or unclear, use non-avatar visuals.

## Should

- Prefer non-avatar visuals unless the task, client profile, or style pack clearly calls for an avatar route.
- Keep avatar delivery low-pressure for outreach or sales contexts.

## Preference

- Use neutral presenter framing for synthetic avatar candidates until a client style pack defines stronger taste.
