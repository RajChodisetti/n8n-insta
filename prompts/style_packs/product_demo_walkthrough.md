# Product Demo Walkthrough

## Best Use

Product flows, feature walkthroughs, workflow explainers, and SaaS demos.

## Poor Use

Emotional brand films, testimonials without product detail, or meme formats.

## Pacing

Clear step-by-step progress with a short hook and benefit close.

## Visuals

Abstract dashboards, blurred UI cards, workflow states, handoff moments, and product-adjacent motion.

## Captions

Benefit and workflow clarity, not feature stuffing.

## Transitions

Slides, morphs, and clean step transitions.

## Music

Clean optimistic synth or light pulse.

## SFX

Soft clicks and state-change accents, never noisy.

## Voice

Calm product walkthrough with plain language.

## Avatar Policy

Presenter optional only if the demo needs a guide. Avoid real likeness without consent.

## Template Needs

- User problem
- Workflow steps
- Key benefit
- Safe product claims

## Negative Rules

- Do not show readable proprietary UI or customer data.
- Do not claim integrations or metrics not in the payload.
