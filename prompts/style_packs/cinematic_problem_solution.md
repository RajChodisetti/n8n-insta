# Cinematic Problem Solution

## Best Use

Narratives that define a clear pain, reveal stakes, and resolve with a product, process, or insight.

## Poor Use

Pure tutorials, jokes, or content where no meaningful transformation exists.

## Pacing

Tension-forward opening, rising friction, clean solution turn, confident close.

## Visuals

Problem state, friction details, contrast frames, and resolved outcome with consistent cinematic realism.

## Captions

Problem then solution phrasing with one concrete promise that the source supports.

## Transitions

Contrast cuts, match cuts, and reveal transitions.

## Music

Subtle cinematic pulse that lifts at the solution turn.

## SFX

Low-impact transition hits and restrained environmental accents.

## Voice

Confident, clear, and slightly dramatic without hype.

## Avatar Policy

Usually no avatar. Use a presenter only when the solution needs an explicit human guide.

## Template Needs

- Problem
- Stakes
- Solution
- Before state
- After state

## Negative Rules

- Do not manufacture urgency or guaranteed results.
- Do not overstate the solution beyond evidence.
