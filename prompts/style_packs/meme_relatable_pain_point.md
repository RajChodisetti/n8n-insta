# Meme Relatable Pain Point

## Best Use

Lightweight pain-point relatability, B2B humor, creator frustrations, and social-native observations.

## Poor Use

Sensitive topics, serious client proof, regulated claims, or trauma stories.

## Pacing

Fast setup, recognizable pain, quick exaggeration, light resolution.

## Visuals

Expressive situations, reaction shots, exaggerated workflow moments, no copyrighted meme templates.

## Captions

Short relatable line, not a long explanation.

## Transitions

Jump cuts, reaction cuts, and quick reversals.

## Music

Playful minimal beat or comedic pulse.

## SFX

Small comedic hits, pauses, and notification textures.

## Voice

Conversational, lightly sarcastic, never mean.

## Avatar Policy

Synthetic avatar allowed for generic character bits. Avoid real-person parody without rights.

## Template Needs

- Relatable pain
- Audience
- Safe exaggeration
- Resolution or wink

## Negative Rules

- Do not punch down or mock customers.
- Do not use copyrighted meme formats or celebrity likenesses.
