# UGC Style Product Pitch

## Best Use

Casual product pitches, creator-style demos, user-perspective benefits, and lightweight social proof.

## Poor Use

Regulated claims, unverified testimonials, premium brand films, or serious crisis topics.

## Pacing

Casual hook, personal problem, product moment, benefit, quick CTA.

## Visuals

Handheld feel, desk or home environment, product-in-use shots, natural light, no fake platform UI.

## Captions

Conversational first-person caption with clear disclosure when sponsored or synthetic.

## Transitions

Handheld cuts, quick zooms, and natural action cuts.

## Music

Light social-native beat that does not overpower voice.

## SFX

Natural product sounds and light taps.

## Voice

Conversational, credible, casual, not overproduced.

## Avatar Policy

Real UGC creator likeness requires consent and disclosure. Synthetic presenter must be transparent.

## Template Needs

- User problem
- Product use
- Allowed claim
- Disclosure status
- CTA

## Negative Rules

- Do not fake testimonials or user results.
- Do not omit sponsorship or synthetic-presenter disclosure when required.
