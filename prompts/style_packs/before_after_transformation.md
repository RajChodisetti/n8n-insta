# Before After Transformation

## Best Use

Workflow improvements, design upgrades, process changes, cleanup stories, and clear transformations.

## Poor Use

Stories where the after state is speculative or unproven.

## Pacing

Fast before state, clear turning point, satisfying after reveal.

## Visuals

Split contrast implied through sequencing, not split panels; messy-to-clean progression.

## Captions

Before, change, after in simple language.

## Transitions

Match cuts, wipes, and reveal cuts that show contrast.

## Music

Build from light tension to clean lift.

## SFX

Small whooshes or reveal hits, restrained.

## Voice

Confident and practical, with momentum.

## Avatar Policy

No avatar by default. Use process visuals first.

## Template Needs

- Before state
- Change made
- After state
- Proof boundary

## Negative Rules

- Do not fake before/after evidence.
- Do not imply a guaranteed transformation.
