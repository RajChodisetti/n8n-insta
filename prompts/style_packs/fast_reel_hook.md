# Fast Reel Hook

## Best Use

Short tactical tips, creator education, quick checks, and high-retention social advice.

## Poor Use

Sensitive stories, reflective founder pieces, or complex topics needing nuance.

## Pacing

Immediate hook, short beats, fast cuts, crisp close.

## Visuals

Phone previews, editing timelines, abstract creator workflow, hands, and punchy motion.

## Captions

Compact action-first captions with one direct instruction.

## Transitions

Jump cuts, snap cuts, whip cuts, and hard cuts.

## Music

Dry percussion, tight clicks, or fast clean pulse.

## SFX

Short taps, swipes, cuts, and edit hits used sparingly.

## Voice

Fast, clipped, direct, and energetic.

## Avatar Policy

No avatar by default. Creator hands or abstract UI are preferred.

## Template Needs

- One tactic
- Why it matters
- 3-4 step flow
- Quick close

## Negative Rules

- Do not promise views, growth, or algorithm outcomes.
- Do not overload scenes with explanation.
