# Founder Explainer

## Best Use

Founder-led lessons, startup decisions, operator reflections, and practical business explanations.

## Poor Use

Highly comedic content, anonymous mystery stories, or posts that need fast meme timing.

## Pacing

Measured hook, steady middle, reflective close.

## Visuals

Founder workspace, notes, product sketches, support queues, abstract dashboards, no readable UI text.

## Captions

Clear lesson-first copy with a practical takeaway.

## Transitions

Simple cuts, push-ins, and match cuts between work artifacts.

## Music

Warm minimal piano or soft pulse under narration.

## SFX

Sparse desk, keyboard, paper, or subtle notification textures.

## Voice

Grounded founder reflection, credible and calm.

## Avatar Policy

Avatar not needed by default. Synthetic presenter is allowed only if transparent and client-approved.

## Template Needs

- Core lesson
- Founder point of view
- Specific operating moment
- Practical takeaway

## Negative Rules

- Do not invent traction, customer names, revenue, or proprietary details.
- Do not show readable customer data or product UI.
