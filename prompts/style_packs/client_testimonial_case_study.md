# Client Testimonial Case Study

## Best Use

Permissioned customer wins, anonymized case studies, and proof-backed service stories.

## Poor Use

Unverified outcomes, private client details, or content without approval to share.

## Pacing

Credibility-first hook, context, action, result, caveated lesson.

## Visuals

Anonymized client context, abstract metrics, team workflow, and proof-safe visuals.

## Captions

Outcome with context and no unsupported guarantee.

## Transitions

Clean documentary cuts and evidence-focused reveals.

## Music

Professional, restrained, optimistic.

## SFX

Minimal; use only for evidence-card movement.

## Voice

Credible case-study narrator, measured and specific.

## Avatar Policy

Real client likeness requires consent. Default to anonymized visuals or synthetic placeholders.

## Template Needs

- Client consent status
- Problem
- Action
- Result
- Allowed proof

## Negative Rules

- Do not reveal private client data.
- Do not invent testimonial quotes or metrics.
