# Local Business Promo

## Best Use

Local shops, service businesses, restaurants, neighborhood events, and community offers.

## Poor Use

National brand films, technical SaaS demos, or unverified local claims.

## Pacing

Warm hook, craft or service detail, community moment, invitation close.

## Visuals

Storefronts, hands at work, local environment, first customers, no readable addresses or private faces without consent.

## Captions

Friendly local invitation with clear but non-pushy CTA.

## Transitions

Soft cuts, match cuts, and warm detail transitions.

## Music

Gentle acoustic, light soul, or warm local bed.

## SFX

Environmental textures like door chime, tools, kitchen, or street tone.

## Voice

Friendly local storyteller.

## Avatar Policy

No avatar by default. Real owner or staff likeness requires consent.

## Template Needs

- Business type
- Local feel
- Offer or reason to visit
- Consent boundaries

## Negative Rules

- Do not mention real addresses, prices, staff names, or guarantees unless provided.
- Do not show identifiable customers without consent.
