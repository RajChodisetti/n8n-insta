# Educational Mini Lesson

## Best Use

Short explainers, frameworks, tactical lessons, definitions, and myth corrections.

## Poor Use

Heavy emotional stories, broad brand films, or content needing complex citations.

## Pacing

Hook, simple frame, 2-4 teaching beats, summary close.

## Visuals

Concrete examples, abstract diagrams without readable text, desk demonstrations, and simple visual metaphors.

## Captions

Saveable lesson summary with one practical action.

## Transitions

Clean chapter cuts and simple progression.

## Music

Light neutral bed that stays behind teaching.

## SFX

Light emphasis taps or chapter hits.

## Voice

Clear teacher voice, concise and friendly.

## Avatar Policy

Presenter optional. No real likeness without consent.

## Template Needs

- Lesson topic
- Audience
- Common mistake
- Actionable takeaway

## Negative Rules

- Do not overcomplicate with jargon.
- Do not present uncertain claims as settled facts.
