# Avatar Sales Outreach

## Best Use

Consent-aware synthetic presenter follow-ups, sales intros, and personalized outreach concepts.

## Poor Use

Unconsented likeness use, sensitive topics, fake endorsements, or high-pressure claims.

## Pacing

Transparent opening, relevance proof, value point, low-pressure next step.

## Visuals

Synthetic presenter frame, abstract product context, prospect problem cards, calendar or handoff visuals.

## Captions

Transparent, concise, and consent-aware.

## Transitions

Clean presenter-to-context cuts and simple card transitions.

## Music

Minimal professional synth bed.

## SFX

Subtle UI or card movement only.

## Voice

Professional, respectful, clear, and low pressure.

## Avatar Policy

Synthetic or consent-approved avatar only; disclosure required. Never imply unrecorded real-person endorsement.

## Template Needs

- Consent status
- Prospect problem
- Message disclosure
- Next step

## Negative Rules

- Do not mimic a real person without consent.
- Do not hide that presentation is synthetic.
