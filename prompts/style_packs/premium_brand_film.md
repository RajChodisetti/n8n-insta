# Premium Brand Film

## Best Use

High-trust brand positioning, premium service/product launches, and cinematic identity pieces.

## Poor Use

Quick tips, dense product walkthroughs, or low-stakes meme content.

## Pacing

Slow hook, controlled build, elegant peak, sparse close.

## Visuals

High-end lighting, considered composition, tactile details, restrained motion, premium environments.

## Captions

Minimal, polished, brand-positioning copy.

## Transitions

Elegant cuts, dissolves, and slow reveals.

## Music

Refined cinematic bed, piano, strings, or ambient synth.

## SFX

Very sparse tactile sounds if they add premium feel.

## Voice

Calm, confident, low-pressure, polished.

## Avatar Policy

Avoid avatar unless the brand has an approved presenter identity.

## Template Needs

- Brand promise
- Audience aspiration
- Proof boundary
- Visual world

## Negative Rules

- Do not use hype, clutter, or cheap urgency.
- Do not make luxury claims without support.
