# 01 — Product Overview

## Product name

Working name: **Instagram AI Storytelling Engine**

## Vision

Create a semi-autonomous system that turns interesting real or legend-adjacent stories into short, high-retention Instagram Reels with minimal human intervention.

## Target output

A polished Instagram Reel package consisting of:
- final 9:16 MP4
- caption
- hashtags
- optional cover image
- metadata record
- post-publish performance report

## Target content style

- English-language storytelling
- faceless channel
- AI-generated narration or synthetic narration
- AI-generated or stock-supported visuals
- cinematic mood
- tension-first structure
- mystery / history / strange true story angle

## Example story categories

- abandoned ships
- mysterious disappearances
- strange survival stories
- unsolved historical incidents
- eerie archaeology stories
- unexplained discoveries
- lost expedition stories

## Audience objective

Attract broad English-speaking Instagram users who enjoy highly watchable short-form mystery/history content.

## Business objective

Build a repeatable content engine that can:
- publish consistently
- improve over time
- reduce manual work
- eventually scale into multiple content themes if desired

## Success criteria

### Content quality
- strong hook in first 1–2 seconds
- clean pacing
- readable subtitles
- visual coherence
- strong ending line

### System quality
- workflows are modular
- failures are easy to trace
- content states are visible
- publishing is trackable
- insights are collected automatically

### Growth quality
- increasing completion rate
- increasing save/share rate
- improved repeatability of successful themes

## Non-goals for v1

- multi-platform publishing
- long-form YouTube content
- creator face-based content
- complex manual editing dependency
- advanced multi-language support
- deep A/B testing infrastructure

## Product principle

This is not just a Reel maker.
This is a **story-to-performance loop**.
